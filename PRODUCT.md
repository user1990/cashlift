# Product

CashLift is a **Company Workspace** for service firms. Company members use it to make daily cash decisions across spend requests, invoices, vendor bills, subscriptions, team budgets, and the 13-week Cash Outlook.

Use `CONTEXT.md` for vocabulary. Do not substitute account, tenant, user, task, or invoice-for-vendor-bill.

## What settings is for

Settings is the company-workspace configuration surface. It shows how this workspace is identified, which default role it is configured for, which cash guardrails it uses, where its records come from, and which product boundaries are in force.

It is not a payment console, payroll runner, or bank. An approved decision may be handed off elsewhere; CashLift does not move or custody money.

## Public demo vs company records

`/demo/workspace` is a read-only Studio Nova tour on checked-in fixtures. `/dashboard` loads company records for an authenticated company workspace. Settings copy must keep those sources distinct.
