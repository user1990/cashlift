Lesson 33 of 40

If your best people do AI-doable work, leadership is the bottleneck

Staff-level point: Staff-level leverage means protecting senior attention.

Real Product / Engineering Example

Do not let staff engineers spend hours formatting release notes, copying logs, or writing first-pass summaries.

Practice Drill

Audit your calendar for AI-doable tasks and choose one to remove this week.

Operating Checklist

  • Define the business or engineering outcome before touching tools.
  • Name the owner, inputs, output, verification, and failure path.
  • Use examples or fixtures when quality matters.
  • Make the model show uncertainty or route to a human when needed.
  • Record the reusable prompt, context pack, or runbook.

Feedback Loop

Pass condition: Senior time moves toward architecture, risk, and people leverage.
