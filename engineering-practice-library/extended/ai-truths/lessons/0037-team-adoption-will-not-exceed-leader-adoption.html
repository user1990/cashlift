Team adoption will not exceed leader adoption

Staff-level point: Leaders set the practical ceiling.

Real Product / Engineering Example

A staff engineer publishes their own AI-assisted review workflow, failures included, so the team can copy and improve it.

Practice Drill

Create a one-page “how I use AI in engineering work” memo with examples and boundaries.

Operating Checklist

  • Define the business or engineering outcome before touching tools.
  • Name the owner, inputs, output, verification, and failure path.
  • Use examples or fixtures when quality matters.
  • Make the model show uncertainty or route to a human when needed.
  • Record the reusable prompt, context pack, or runbook.

Feedback Loop

Pass condition: Team sees real behavior, not slogans.
