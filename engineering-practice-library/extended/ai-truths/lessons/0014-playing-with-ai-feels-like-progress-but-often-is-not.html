Lesson 14 of 40

Playing with AI feels like progress, but often is not

Staff-level point: Novel tools are not outcomes. Tie exploration to a metric.

Real Product / Engineering Example

Instead of trying five agent frameworks, reduce “time from ticket to reviewed PR” by 30%.

Practice Drill

Create an experiment card: hypothesis, metric, baseline, time-box, artifact, kill criteria.

Operating Checklist

  • Define the business or engineering outcome before touching tools.
  • Name the owner, inputs, output, verification, and failure path.
  • Use examples or fixtures when quality matters.
  • Make the model show uncertainty or route to a human when needed.
  • Record the reusable prompt, context pack, or runbook.

Feedback Loop

Pass condition: Experiment ends with a shipped workflow or a clear no-go.

Quick retrieval: What should you do before scaling this lesson into a production workflow?
