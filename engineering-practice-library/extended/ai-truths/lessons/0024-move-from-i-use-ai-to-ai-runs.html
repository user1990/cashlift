Move from “I use AI” to “AI runs”

Staff-level point: The leap is from prompting to orchestrated workflows.

Real Product / Engineering Example

A task automation watches issue labels, reads context, proposes implementation plan, creates branch, runs tests, and asks for approval before PR.

Practice Drill

Draw one end-to-end workflow with trigger, tools, state, human checkpoints, and rollback.

Operating Checklist

  • Define the business or engineering outcome before touching tools.
  • Name the owner, inputs, output, verification, and failure path.
  • Use examples or fixtures when quality matters.
  • Make the model show uncertainty or route to a human when needed.
  • Record the reusable prompt, context pack, or runbook.

Feedback Loop

Pass condition: Human reviews decisions, not every copy-paste step.
