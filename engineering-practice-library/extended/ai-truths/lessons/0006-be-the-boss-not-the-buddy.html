Lesson 6 of 40

Be the boss, not the buddy

Staff-level point: Direct constraints outperform vague politeness. Systems need rules, not vibes.

Real Product / Engineering Example

Write coding-agent instructions: never change unrelated files, always run targeted tests, stop on destructive commands, report exact verification.

Practice Drill

Draft a command-style instruction block for auto code review or task automation. Include 5 “never” rules.

Operating Checklist

  • Define the business or engineering outcome before touching tools.
  • Name the owner, inputs, output, verification, and failure path.
  • Use examples or fixtures when quality matters.
  • Make the model show uncertainty or route to a human when needed.
  • Record the reusable prompt, context pack, or runbook.

Feedback Loop

Pass condition: Rules are short, imperative, and testable.
