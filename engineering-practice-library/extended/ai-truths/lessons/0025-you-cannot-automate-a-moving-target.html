Lesson 25 of 40

You cannot automate a moving target

Staff-level point: Automate stable interfaces, not chaotic experiments.

Real Product / Engineering Example

Do not automate a product discovery process that changes weekly; automate stable bug report normalization.

Practice Drill

Split one workflow into stable, changing, and unknown steps. Automate only stable steps.

Operating Checklist

  • Define the business or engineering outcome before touching tools.
  • Name the owner, inputs, output, verification, and failure path.
  • Use examples or fixtures when quality matters.
  • Make the model show uncertainty or route to a human when needed.
  • Record the reusable prompt, context pack, or runbook.

Feedback Loop

Pass condition: Automation survives process changes.
