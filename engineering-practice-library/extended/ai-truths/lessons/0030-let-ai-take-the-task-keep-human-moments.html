Lesson 30 of 40

Let AI take the task; keep human moments

Staff-level point: Automate toil; keep judgment, trust, and accountability human.

Real Product / Engineering Example

AI summarizes incidents, drafts customer updates, and clusters logs; human owns apology, decision, and follow-through.

Practice Drill

Sort tasks into automate, assist, and human-only for one engineering/product workflow.

Operating Checklist

  • Define the business or engineering outcome before touching tools.
  • Name the owner, inputs, output, verification, and failure path.
  • Use examples or fixtures when quality matters.
  • Make the model show uncertainty or route to a human when needed.
  • Record the reusable prompt, context pack, or runbook.

Feedback Loop

Pass condition: High-empathy or high-risk decisions stay human-led.
