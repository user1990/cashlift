A tool with no owner is a toy

Staff-level point: Operational ownership turns AI experiments into systems.

Real Product / Engineering Example

Assign DRI for code-review bot: prompt changes, false positives, escalation, weekly quality review.

Practice Drill

Write an ownership card for one workflow: owner, backup, metric, runbook, review cadence.

Operating Checklist

  • Define the business or engineering outcome before touching tools.
  • Name the owner, inputs, output, verification, and failure path.
  • Use examples or fixtures when quality matters.
  • Make the model show uncertainty or route to a human when needed.
  • Record the reusable prompt, context pack, or runbook.

Feedback Loop

Pass condition: Someone is accountable when output degrades.
