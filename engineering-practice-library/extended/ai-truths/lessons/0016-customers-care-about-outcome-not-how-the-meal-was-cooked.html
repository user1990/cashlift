Lesson 16 of 40

Customers care about outcome, not how the meal was cooked

Staff-level point: AI is a production technique; quality and trust still belong to you.

Real Product / Engineering Example

Use AI to draft customer onboarding workflows, but measure onboarding completion and customer satisfaction.

Practice Drill

Pick a product deliverable and define which parts AI may draft, which parts require human approval, and what quality bar matters.

Operating Checklist

  • Define the business or engineering outcome before touching tools.
  • Name the owner, inputs, output, verification, and failure path.
  • Use examples or fixtures when quality matters.
  • Make the model show uncertainty or route to a human when needed.
  • Record the reusable prompt, context pack, or runbook.

Feedback Loop

Pass condition: Outcome improves without hiding risk or lowering quality.
