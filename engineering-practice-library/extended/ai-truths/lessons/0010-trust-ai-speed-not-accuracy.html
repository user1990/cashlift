Trust AI speed, not accuracy

Staff-level point: Fast wrong work is still wrong. Verification is part of the workflow.

Real Product / Engineering Example

Coding agent writes patch; verification requires tests, lint, typecheck, and diff review before merge.

Practice Drill

Design a verification ladder: cheap checks first, expensive checks later, human approval at risk gates.

Operating Checklist

  • Define the business or engineering outcome before touching tools.
  • Name the owner, inputs, output, verification, and failure path.
  • Use examples or fixtures when quality matters.
  • Make the model show uncertainty or route to a human when needed.
  • Record the reusable prompt, context pack, or runbook.

Feedback Loop

Pass condition: No AI output is accepted without matching verification.
