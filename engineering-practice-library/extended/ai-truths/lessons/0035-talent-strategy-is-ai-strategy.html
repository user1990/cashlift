Talent strategy is AI strategy

Staff-level point: AI-native hiring evaluates workflow judgment, not tool name-dropping.

Real Product / Engineering Example

Interview asks candidate to use AI to review a PR, explain verification, and identify where not to trust the model.

Practice Drill

Design an interview exercise for an AI-native staff engineer or automation engineer.

Operating Checklist

  • Define the business or engineering outcome before touching tools.
  • Name the owner, inputs, output, verification, and failure path.
  • Use examples or fixtures when quality matters.
  • Make the model show uncertainty or route to a human when needed.
  • Record the reusable prompt, context pack, or runbook.

Feedback Loop

Pass condition: Exercise tests judgment, verification, and systems thinking.
