If AI is not moving a number, it is just a hobby

Staff-level point: Staff engineers make AI measurable.

Real Product / Engineering Example

Track PR review latency, escaped defects, cycle time, support response time, cost per task, or revenue pipeline impact.

Practice Drill

Choose one AI workflow and write baseline, target, owner, measurement interval, and stop condition.

Operating Checklist

  • Define the business or engineering outcome before touching tools.
  • Name the owner, inputs, output, verification, and failure path.
  • Use examples or fixtures when quality matters.
  • Make the model show uncertainty or route to a human when needed.
  • Record the reusable prompt, context pack, or runbook.

Feedback Loop

Pass condition: A real metric changes or the workflow gets killed.
