AI will not just replace people; it can free them

Staff-level point: Role redesign is the real leverage.

Real Product / Engineering Example

A senior engineer stops manually summarizing PRs and spends more time designing architecture and mentoring.

Practice Drill

Pick one role and redesign its week after AI removes repetitive tasks.

Operating Checklist

  • Define the business or engineering outcome before touching tools.
  • Name the owner, inputs, output, verification, and failure path.
  • Use examples or fixtures when quality matters.
  • Make the model show uncertainty or route to a human when needed.
  • Record the reusable prompt, context pack, or runbook.

Feedback Loop

Pass condition: Freed time is reassigned to higher-value work.
