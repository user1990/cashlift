Barrier is willingness to try

Staff-level point: Repeated attempts build the skill.

Real Product / Engineering Example

Run weekly failed-output reviews: what context, example, constraint, or verification was missing?

Practice Drill

Create a prompt/workflow journal with failed attempt, diagnosis, improved version, and reusable lesson.

Operating Checklist

  • Define the business or engineering outcome before touching tools.
  • Name the owner, inputs, output, verification, and failure path.
  • Use examples or fixtures when quality matters.
  • Make the model show uncertainty or route to a human when needed.
  • Record the reusable prompt, context pack, or runbook.

Feedback Loop

Pass condition: Failures become reusable engineering knowledge.
