Lesson 7 of 40

Stop typing; start talking to AI

Staff-level point: Voice is useful for capturing tacit process knowledge before turning it into automation.

Real Product / Engineering Example

Record yourself explaining how you triage a production bug; convert transcript into an incident triage playbook.

Practice Drill

Voice-dump a recurring workflow for 3 minutes. Ask AI to extract steps, decisions, data sources, and failure points.

Operating Checklist

  • Define the business or engineering outcome before touching tools.
  • Name the owner, inputs, output, verification, and failure path.
  • Use examples or fixtures when quality matters.
  • Make the model show uncertainty or route to a human when needed.
  • Record the reusable prompt, context pack, or runbook.

Feedback Loop

Pass condition: Transcript becomes a usable SOP or automation spec.
