AI is not a department; it is a way of operating

Staff-level point: Every function needs operational AI patterns.

Real Product / Engineering Example

Product uses AI for discovery synthesis, engineering for review, support for routing, finance for anomaly detection.

Practice Drill

Map one company function to one AI workflow, owner, metric, and source of truth.

Operating Checklist

  • Define the business or engineering outcome before touching tools.
  • Name the owner, inputs, output, verification, and failure path.
  • Use examples or fixtures when quality matters.
  • Make the model show uncertainty or route to a human when needed.
  • Record the reusable prompt, context pack, or runbook.
