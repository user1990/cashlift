AI sounds like a stranger when you never introduced yourself

Staff-level point: Reusable style, architecture, and decision context make outputs feel native to your team.

Real Product / Engineering Example

Create a “repo voice” pack: architecture principles, review style, naming conventions, test habits, product language.

Practice Drill

Build a project context pack for one repo: conventions, patterns, commands, anti-patterns, and preferred review tone.

Operating Checklist

  • Define the business or engineering outcome before touching tools.
  • Name the owner, inputs, output, verification, and failure path.
  • Use examples or fixtures when quality matters.
  • Make the model show uncertainty or route to a human when needed.
  • Record the reusable prompt, context pack, or runbook.

Feedback Loop
