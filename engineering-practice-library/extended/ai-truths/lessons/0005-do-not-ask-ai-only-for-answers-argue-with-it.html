Lesson 5 of 40

Do not ask AI only for answers; argue with it

Staff-level point: Use AI as a design-review opponent, not a consensus machine.

Real Product / Engineering Example

Ask an AI reviewer to attack your proposed workflow: data risk, failure mode, hidden dependency, bad metric, security hole, owner gap.

Practice Drill

Take one product automation idea and run a red-team prompt against it. Convert top 3 objections into design changes.

Operating Checklist

  • Define the business or engineering outcome before touching tools.
  • Name the owner, inputs, output, verification, and failure path.
  • Use examples or fixtures when quality matters.
  • Make the model show uncertainty or route to a human when needed.
  • Record the reusable prompt, context pack, or runbook.

Feedback Loop

Pass condition: The final design is stronger because objections changed it.
