Lesson 39 of 40

AI has no identity problem; people do

Staff-level point: The obstacle is often control, pride, or fear of delegation.

Real Product / Engineering Example

Let AI create the first draft of an architecture note, but keep final judgment and ownership.

Practice Drill

Choose one task you resist delegating to AI. Let AI draft only version one, then edit aggressively.

Operating Checklist

  • Define the business or engineering outcome before touching tools.
  • Name the owner, inputs, output, verification, and failure path.
  • Use examples or fixtures when quality matters.
  • Make the model show uncertainty or route to a human when needed.
  • Record the reusable prompt, context pack, or runbook.

Feedback Loop

Pass condition: You separate identity from output ownership.
