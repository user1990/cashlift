Prompting Reference

Use prompts like executable specs.

Use This Checklist

  • Role: what expert perspective should the model use?
  • Objective: what exact artifact or decision is needed?
  • Inputs: what source material is allowed?
  • Constraints: what must never happen?
  • Examples: what does good output look like?
  • Output: what schema or format should return?
