C14

Internationalization: formats, language, pluralization

Internationalization is not translation sprinkled at the end; it is product correctness across locales.

Sprint index

The rule

Externalize text, use locale-aware date/number/currency APIs, and handle pluralization and text expansion.

Example

A vehicle price, date, mileage unit, and legal disclaimer may all change by locale.

Practice

Take one UI card and identify every string, date, number, currency, unit, and plural form.

Write a concrete answer, then check.

Good answer direction

Strong answer: I design i18n as a data and layout constraint from the start, especially for regulated or trust-heavy UI.
