WebGL / WebGPU: GPU-backed rendering

GPU APIs are useful when the UI is graphics-bound, not just because the result looks advanced.

Sprint index

The rule

Reach for WebGL/WebGPU when canvas/SVG/DOM cannot meet rendering volume, frame rate, or visual requirements.

Example

A dense damage heatmap or 3D vehicle view may justify GPU rendering; ordinary dashboards usually do not.

Practice

Choose one visual. Decide DOM, SVG, Canvas, WebGL, or WebGPU and explain the tradeoff.

Write a concrete answer, then check.

Good answer direction

Strong answer: I choose the rendering technology by data volume, interaction needs, accessibility impact, and fallback cost.
