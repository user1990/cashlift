Quick reference

Senior vs Staff

Level is demonstrated by repeated scope and leverage, not a title or a single large project.

DimensionSenior evidenceStaff evidence
ProblemComplex domain or journeySystemic constraint across domains
DirectionImproves local designSets adaptable technical direction
AdoptionOwn team uses solutionMultiple teams adopt with migration paths
AmbiguityClarifies feature deliveryFrames organization-level problem
LeadershipMentors and reviewsAligns, sponsors, and multiplies
Time horizonRelease and domain healthLong-term platform and business health
SuccessReliable domain outcomeBetter decisions and throughput around them

Staff test: If this person left, would only code output decline—or would technical direction, cross-team decisions, and other engineers’ effectiveness also degrade?

Four proof questions

  1. Which teams changed behavior?
  2. What adoption path made change feasible?
  3. Which disagreement or trade-off did you resolve?
  4. What durable capability remained after delivery?
