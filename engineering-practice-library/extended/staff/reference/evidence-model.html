Quick reference

The evidence chain

A compact test for every resume bullet and interview story.

Context → Action → Mechanism → Outcome → Scope

Not every sentence needs all five elements, but the reader must be able to reconstruct them without guessing.

ElementQuestionEvidence
ContextWhat material problem existed?Checkout abandonment; fragmented auth; slow releases
ActionWhat did you personally drive?Designed; led; migrated; established; investigated
MechanismWhy did the change work?State machine; shared contract; rollout guardrail; RUM
OutcomeWhat changed?Conversion; failures; incidents; lead time; adoption
ScopeHow far did it reach?Markets; applications; journeys; teams; users

Credibility test

Attribution: Can you separate your contribution from the team result?

Baseline: Compared with what starting point?

Method: How was the outcome measured?

Trade-off: What risk or cost did you manage?

Verb ladder

Weak: helped, participated, worked on

Precise: diagnosed, designed, led, standardized, migrated, instrumented, reduced

One-line template

[Action] [system] across [scope] by [mechanism], changing [baseline] to [outcome] while [risk/trade-off].
