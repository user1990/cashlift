Lesson 04 · Context

A weekend app is not a mature codebase

Agents can look miraculous on a blank folder. The same tools look merely helpful in a product with users, history, and review. Classify the task before you prompt.

Greenfield vs brownfieldOne retrieval checkNo setup required

Win for this lesson

Before prompting, you can label a task greenfield or brownfield and predict which pipeline step will dominate.

Do not clash

This is not prompt craft (40 AI Truths) and not “direct the architecture delta” (Direct the system). Classify the terrain, then use those courses for the work.

GreenfieldBrownfield
What existsFew invariants, few usersHistory, owners, incidents, compatibility
Where AI looks fastScaffold, UI, happy pathLocal boilerplate inside a known module
Where time goesChoosing what to build; later, usersAlignment, review, verification, rollout
False conclusion“We can replace the SaaS this weekend”“Agents do not help”

Large-company feature pace often barely moves even when diffs get larger, because generation was not the constraint. A vibe-coded prototype can still be the right tool—if you do not confuse it with production throughput.

Retrieval check

What should you do before asking an agent to implement a change?

Make it yours

Take your next three tickets or side-project tasks. Label each G or B. For each, name the step that will dominate elapsed time.

Practice note

Same model, different constraint

Keep METR-style caveats in mind: studies on experienced developers in familiar mature repos are about brownfield. Do not average them with “I scaffolded an app on Saturday.”

Ask your teacher

Bring the real artifact from the drill. The lesson is only complete when it maps onto a repo, PR, or product you own.
