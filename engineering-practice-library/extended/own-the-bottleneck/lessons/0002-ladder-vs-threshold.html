Lesson 02 · Career

The missing rungs are the assignment

When low-complexity work is generated, a ladder career becomes a threshold career. Mid and senior work remains; the on-the-job training path does not.

Career modelOne retrieval checkNo setup required

Win for this lesson

You can explain the ladder-to-threshold shift without quoting rotting job-market statistics, and name one way you will manufacture a missing rung.

Do not clash

This is not Senior / Staff readiness and not interview question banks. Those courses score ownership and frontend judgment. This lesson only names why junior-shaped work thinned out and what that implies for practice.

Two shapes

L

Ladder

Entry work exists because someone must type the obvious slice. You step: intern → junior → mid → senior. Training is bundled with the grunt work.

T

Threshold

The obvious slice is cheap to generate. Hiring concentrates above a bar: people who can design, align, verify, and ship. Below the bar, openings shrink even if the field still grows overall.

The paradox is real: if organizations stop training juniors, they still need mids and seniors later. Waiting for a classic junior ticket is not a strategy. Manufacturing evidence of threshold work is.

What “threshold work” looks like

Below the barAcross the bar
Implement a sketched ticket exactlyName the constraint, slice, and proof before generating
Add features until the repo feels donePut a build in front of a user and record what changed
Compete with the model at syntaxOwn design, alignment, verification systems, and release risk

Do not paste unemployment rates, hiring-index drops, or layoff headlines into this course. Those numbers age in months. Keep the model: the cheap layer moved; the expensive layer did not disappear.

Retrieval check

If junior tickets disappear, what is the useful next move?

Make it yours

List three tasks you used to call “junior.” For each, write the pipeline step it actually sat in (design, build, verify, ship, users). Circle one step you can practice this month without a job title.

Do not freeze

Market screenshots are not the lesson

If you track CS-grad unemployment or posting volume, treat them as dated snapshots. The durable claim is the shape of the career, not a percentage from a slide.

Ask your teacher

Bring the real artifact from the drill. The lesson is only complete when it maps onto a repo, PR, or product you own.
