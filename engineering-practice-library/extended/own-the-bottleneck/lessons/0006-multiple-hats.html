Lesson 06 · Practice

A pure coder is the replaceable slice

If you only offer typing, you compete where generation is strongest. Fluid movement across the pipeline is the meta-skill: unblock the constraint, not the compiler.

Multi-hat practiceOne retrieval checkPortfolio evidence

Win for this lesson

You can plan one project week as rotating hats (build, verify, product, research, distribute) and describe that evidence as threshold-level work.

Do not clash

Do not rebuild verification systems here—use practical guardrails and the smallest gauntlet. Do not start the staff interview rubric. Hats are how you manufacture the missing ladder rungs.

Hats that match the pipeline

PMProductWhat is in / out this week
REResearchWatch one user; write facts
ENBuildGenerate inside a boundary
QAVerifyChecks and stop conditions
GTDistributeShow it; ask for the next fact

Collaboration counts. A three-person weekend that reaches production is stronger evidence than a private tutorial clone. You need stories about conflict, tradeoffs, and release—not only a GitHub heatmap.

When generation is cheap, the scarce person is the one who notices the pile-up and moves to the blocked step.

Make it yours

Plan five weekdays for one project. Assign a hat to each day. On verify day, link the gauntlet instead of inventing a new QA philosophy.
