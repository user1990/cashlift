Adopt the smallest useful gauntlet

A quality pipeline earns its place by reducing accepted-change time and escaped defects—not by having the most agents or the strictest numbers.

Vertical pilotRatchet strategyMeasured adoption

Win for this lesson

You will leave with a one-story pilot plan, explicit human checkpoints, and measurements that can justify—or reject—more automation.

The practical quality gauntlet

01SpecifyStory, rules, examples, risks, QA.
02ImplementCode, types, focused tests.
03CleanNames, cohesion, complexity, diff.
04HardenSurvivors, security, boundaries.
05AcceptQA outcome and human judgment.

These are responsibilities, not mandatory agents. One person and one agent can perform all five. Split a role only when independent context, parallelism, or a fresh reviewer measurably improves the outcome.

Human checkpoints

CheckpointHuman ownsAgent can accelerate
Before implementationUser value, policy, risk, acceptance oracleExamples, edge-case proposals, repository discovery

Your one-story pilot

Ratchet; do not demand instant perfection

Legacy repositories often cannot pass an idealized global threshold. Start by preventing regression on changed code. Tighten only after the signal proves useful.

Fast loop on each change
  build / typecheck
  focused tests
  lint / static analysis
  architecture rules

Review loop on the changed slice
  coverage gaps explained
  selected mutation survivors killed or classified
  human diff and outcome review

Acceptance loop
  executable or manual QA at the boundary
  security / accessibility / performance checks by risk
  explicit human acceptance

Measure the system, not the demo

MeasureWhy it matters
Accepted lead timeIncludes implementation, repair loops, review, and QA.
Human attention minutesHuman time is often the scarce resource automation should protect.
Rework loopsReveals vague intent, weak tests, or agent thrashing.
Escaped defectsPrevents “faster merge” from masquerading as productivity.
Unnecessary codeTracks speculative abstractions and cleanup burden.

Stop adding stages when

The check is flaky, its verdict cannot be explained, false positives dominate, the same evidence already exists elsewhere, or its repair cost exceeds the defects it prevents. Automation is part of the product and must justify its maintenance.

Retrieval check

A five-agent pipeline takes longer than one focused agent. What determines whether it is still worthwhile?

Capstone: define your pilot

Write the repository, story, three existing checks, one new check, human acceptance step, and the four measurements you will record.
